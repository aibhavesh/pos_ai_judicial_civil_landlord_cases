🚀 **Excited to share my latest project: AI Judicial Assistant — A POC for Civil & Landlord Cases!** ⚖️

The Indian legal system handles millions of civil and landlord disputes every year — yet legal research still largely relies on manual review of past judgments, which is time-consuming and resource-intensive. I wanted to explore how AI could change that.

**What I built:**
An AI-powered legal analysis tool that uses **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** to help advocates and judges analyse civil & landlord cases against a knowledge base of real Indian judgments — and generate a full strategic legal report in seconds.

**How it works:**
🔍 Paste any case document (plaint, notice, written statement) into the app
🧠 The system retrieves semantically similar past judgments using **FAISS vector search** + **Google Embeddings**
📝 **Google Gemini 1.5 Flash** synthesises the context and generates a structured report covering:
- Case Summary & Key Facts
- Applicable Indian Laws & Sections
- Analysis of Similar Past Judgments
- Likely Judge Questions, Opposing Arguments & Counter-Strategies
- Final Legal Recommendations & Risk Analysis

**Tech Stack:**
⚙️ Streamlit · LangChain · Google Gemini 1.5 Flash · FAISS · Google Embeddings

**Why this matters:**
This POC demonstrates that AI can meaningfully assist legal professionals — not by replacing their judgment, but by accelerating research and surfacing relevant precedents faster. The same architecture can scale to thousands of cases and multiple practice areas.

**What's next:**
📄 PDF upload support · 🌐 Multi-language support (Hindi, Marathi) · 📚 Expanded case knowledge base · 🔗 Citation tracker for exact judgment references
